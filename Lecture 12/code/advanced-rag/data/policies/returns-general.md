# Returns and Refunds — General Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every product sold by Northwind Audio through its own storefront,
unless a product-specific policy says otherwise. It covers the standard return window,
the condition an item must be in, the items we never accept back, and the way a return
is started. Product-specific policies for electronics, damaged goods, exchanges and
refunds are published separately and take precedence over this document when they apply.

## Return window

Unopened items may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. The
30 days are counted from the delivery date recorded by the carrier, not from the order
date and not from the day the parcel is opened. Returns received after the 30-day
window are declined automatically by the returns portal, and the customer is asked to
contact support if they believe the delivery date is wrong.

## Condition requirements

An item must be unused, in its original packaging, and complete with every accessory,
manual and promotional insert that shipped with it. Opened items are accepted only when
they are faulty, and then only under the damaged-goods or warranty policy. We do not
accept returns on the basis that the customer changed their mind after opening the box.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, digital downloads, clearance items marked final sale, and hygiene products
whose seal has been broken are not returnable. Headphones and earbuds are returnable
only if the hygiene seal on the ear cushions is intact, or if the item is faulty.

## How to start a return

Start every return in the customer account under Orders → Return. The portal issues a
return authorisation number and a prepaid label. Write the authorisation number on the
outside of the parcel; parcels without one are refused at the warehouse. Refunds are
paid to the original payment method once the warehouse has inspected the item.

## Seasonal and promotional purchases

Items bought during a seasonal promotion follow the same 30-day window as everything
else, and the refund is calculated at the price actually paid rather than the current
list price. Where a promotion required a minimum basket value and the return drops the
basket below that value, the promotional discount is recovered from the refund; this is
shown on the return confirmation so the customer sees the arithmetic before the parcel
is sent. Free gifts attached to a promotion must be returned with the main item, or
their value is deducted.

## Return authorisation validity

A return authorisation number is valid for 14 days from the day it is issued. Parcels
that arrive after the authorisation has expired are held for five business days and
then returned to the customer at their own cost, because the warehouse cannot process
stock against a closed authorisation. If the customer needs longer, support can issue a
replacement authorisation as long as the original return window has not closed.

## Related policies

Electronics follow a shorter window and a restocking fee. Damaged goods must be reported
quickly and are handled by a separate team. Exchanges have their own window. Refund
processing times are published separately so that support can quote them accurately.
