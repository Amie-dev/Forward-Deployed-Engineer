# Exchanges Policy

## Exchange window

An item may be exchanged within **14 days** of delivery, provided it is unopened and in
its original packaging. The exchange window is shorter than the 30-day return window
because an exchange reserves stock before the returned unit has been inspected. After
14 days the customer can still return the item for a refund if it is inside the return
window, and buy the alternative separately.

## Value rules

Exchanges must be for an item of equal or greater value. When the replacement costs more,
the customer pays the difference at the time the exchange is approved. When it costs
less, the difference is refunded to the original payment method rather than held as
store credit. Price differences caused by a promotion that has since ended are calculated
at the price paid, not the current price.

## Electronics and clearance

Electronics may be exchanged only while the 7-day electronics return window is open, and
the 15% restocking fee is waived for exchanges of the same model in a different colour.
Clearance items marked final sale cannot be exchanged at all.

## Size and variant rules

An exchange may change the size, colour or variant of the same model, and no fee
applies. Changing to a different model is treated as a return followed by a new order,
because the warranty and the price are attached to the model. Where the requested
variant is out of stock, the reservation is held for 72 hours and then released, and
the customer is offered a refund instead.

## Exchange shipping

The replacement is shipped with the same delivery speed as the original order, at no
extra cost when the exchange is caused by a fault or a wrong item. For a change of
mind the customer pays the return shipping of EUR 4.95, and the replacement is shipped
only after the original has been received and inspected.

## Faulty items

A faulty item is never handled as an exchange. It is handled under the damaged-goods
policy in the first 48 hours, and under the manufacturer warranty afterwards, so that the
replacement carries a documented warranty start date.

## How to start an exchange

Choose Orders → Return, select "exchange", and pick the replacement variant. The portal
reserves the replacement for 72 hours while the original is in transit. If the original
does not arrive within that window the reservation is released and the customer is asked
to start again.
