# Refund Processing and Timing

## Timeline

Once the warehouse has inspected a returned item, the refund is issued within **5 to 7
business days**. The money is returned to the original payment method only: the card that
was charged, the bank account used for the transfer, or the gift card that was redeemed.
We cannot refund to a different card, and we cannot pay a refund in cash.

## When the clock starts

The 5 to 7 business days start when the inspection is completed, not when the parcel is
dropped off and not when it is delivered to the warehouse. Inspection usually happens
within two business days of arrival, so the realistic end-to-end time from dropping the
parcel off is roughly 7 to 9 business days. Support should quote the inspection date from
the order timeline rather than guessing.

## Deductions

Any restocking fee is deducted before the refund is issued, so the amount the customer
sees is the amount they receive. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the
return was caused by a fault, a transit-damage claim or a wrong item. Express shipping
upgrades are never refunded.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund is issued when accessories, manuals or promotional inserts are missing,
when the packaging is damaged beyond normal handling, or when the serial number does not
match the original shipment. The partial amount is calculated by the inspection team and
recorded on the order with a reason code.

## Refund methods

Refunds go back to the original payment method only. A card payment is refunded to the
same card, a bank transfer to the same account, and a gift card to the same gift card
code. Where a gift card has expired, the balance is reissued as a new gift card rather
than paid in cash. Refunds are never issued to a different person, even with the
cardholder's written consent, because the payment provider rejects the transaction.

## Chargebacks and disputes

If a customer opens a chargeback before the refund has been issued, the refund process
is paused and the case is handed to the payments team. Support should not promise a
refund date while a dispute is open, because the bank, not Northwind Audio, controls
the timeline. Once the dispute is withdrawn the refund is reissued within the normal 5
to 7 business days.

## If the refund is late

If more than 7 business days have passed since inspection and no refund is visible, the
customer's bank may still be settling the transaction: card refunds can take a further
3 to 5 business days to appear on a statement. Support should confirm the refund
reference from the payment provider before opening a payment investigation.
