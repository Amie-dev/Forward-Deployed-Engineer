# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery definition

A parcel counts as delivered when the carrier records a delivery scan, not when the
customer finds it. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days inside the EU and 5 to 9
business days to the UK and Switzerland. Express delivery is next business day for orders
placed before 14:00 CET.

## Signature and safe place

Orders above EUR 200 require a signature. Below that threshold the carrier may leave the
parcel in a safe place if the customer selected one at checkout, and the delivery scan is
still the delivery date used by every returns window.

## Lost parcels

A parcel with no scan for 7 business days is treated as lost. Support opens a carrier
investigation, and a replacement is shipped without waiting for the investigation to
close when the customer has a clean claims history. Lost-parcel replacements inherit the
warranty of the original order.

## Address changes

An address can be changed until the parcel is handed to the carrier. After that the
customer must use the carrier's redirect service, and any delay caused by a redirect does
not extend the return window.

## Delivery attempts

The carrier attempts delivery twice. After the second failed attempt the parcel is held
at a pickup point for seven days and then returned to the warehouse, which ends the
delivery and starts the return of the order. The delivery scan from the second failed
attempt is the date used for every returns window, so a customer who collects a parcel
late still has the full window from that scan.

## Carrier partners

Standard deliveries are handled by the national postal service inside the EU and by a
commercial carrier elsewhere. Express deliveries always use the commercial carrier.
Tracking numbers are issued at dispatch, and a tracking number that shows no movement
for 7 business days is the trigger for a lost-parcel investigation.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free above EUR 50 and costs EUR 4.95 below that. Return shipping is
free when the return is caused by a fault or a transit-damage claim, and costs EUR 4.95
otherwise, deducted from the refund.
