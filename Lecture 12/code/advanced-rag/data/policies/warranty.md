# Manufacturer Warranty

## Coverage by product

The AX-204 over-ear headphones carry a **12-month** manufacturer warranty. The AX-210
studio monitors carry 24 months. The BX-900 wireless earbuds carry **24 months** because
they are sold as a premium product. Batteries and charging cases are covered for 6
months, and consumable parts such as ear tips are not covered at all.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use: a driver that
stops producing sound, a hinge that fails, a charging port that no longer makes contact,
and Bluetooth radios that drop connections under ordinary conditions. It does not cover
accidental damage, liquid ingress, cosmetic wear, or damage caused by unauthorised
repair.

## Warranty start date

The warranty starts on the delivery date of the original purchase, not on the date of a
replacement. A replacement unit inherits the remaining term of the original warranty
rather than restarting it, which is why the replacement is recorded against the original
order number.

## How to claim

Open Orders → Report a problem, choose "warranty", and describe the fault. The support
team may ask for a short video of the fault because a recording distinguishes a dead
driver from a pairing problem. Approved claims are resolved with a repair, a refurbished
replacement, or a refund at Northwind Audio's discretion, depending on the model and the
remaining term.

## Proof of purchase

A warranty claim requires proof of purchase: an order number from the Northwind Audio
store, or a receipt from an authorised reseller. Where the customer has neither, the
serial number is used to look up the shipment date, and the warranty is counted from
that date. Products bought second-hand are not covered, because the warranty is not
transferable to a new owner.

## Repairs and refurbished units

An approved repair is carried out by an authorised service partner and carries a
90-day guarantee on the repair itself. Where a repair is not economic, a refurbished
unit of the same model is offered instead. A refurbished replacement inherits the
remaining term of the original warranty, and support should state the original delivery
date when explaining the remaining coverage, because customers often assume a
replacement restarts the clock.

## Relationship to returns

The warranty is separate from the return window. A customer outside the 7-day electronics
window, and outside the 48-hour damage window, can still claim under warranty for a
genuine manufacturing defect, and the restocking fee does not apply to warranty claims.
