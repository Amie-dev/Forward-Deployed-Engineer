# Electronics Returns Policy (2025 — superseded)

> This version applied to orders delivered between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2025.
> It is kept for questions about historical orders. It is replaced by the 2026 policy,
> which shortened the electronics window and introduced the restocking fee.

## Return window in 2025

Electronics could be returned within **14 days** of delivery in 2025. The general 30-day
window did not apply to electronics, but the category window was twice as long as it is
today. Orders delivered in 2025 keep the 14-day window even when the return is processed
in 2026, because the policy in force at delivery governs the order.

## Restocking fee in 2025

No restocking fee was charged for electronics returns in 2025. The fee was introduced on
1 January 2026 together with the shorter window, and it never applies retroactively to an
order placed under the 2025 terms.

## Condition requirements in 2025

The condition requirements were the same as today: unopened, sealed, with an intact
hygiene seal on the ear cushions, and a serial number that matches the shipment. Faulty
items were handled under the damaged-goods policy or the warranty, exactly as now.

## Transition rules

An order delivered in 2025 is governed by this 2025 policy for the whole of its
lifetime, including warranty questions asked in 2026. Support should check the delivery
date before quoting a window, and should say which policy version the answer comes
from, because the 14-day and 7-day windows are different enough to change the outcome
of a real case. The same rule applies in the other direction: an order delivered on or
after 1 January 2026 never gets the longer 2025 window, even if the customer asks
during a goodwill conversation.

## Why this document is retained

Support receives questions about 2025 orders for years after the window closes, and the
answer depends on the policy that was in force at delivery. Retrieval must therefore be
able to return a superseded policy when the question is explicitly about a past date, and
must not return it for a question about today.
